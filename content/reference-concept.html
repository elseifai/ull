The Auremont philosophy

Find the gold.
Help every child climb.

Auremont brings together aurum, gold, and mont, mountain. Our purpose is to recognise the potential in each child and develop it through thoughtful teaching, practice and encouragement.

01 /

Understand deeply.

Build the why and the how. Connect concepts to real situations, with support for children who need it and deeper challenges for those ready to stretch.

02 /

Stay rooted.

Explore Indian thinkers, Karnataka's culture, languages and local examples alongside knowledge from around the world.

03 /

Move with purpose.

Develop movement, fitness, teamwork and fair play through an age-appropriate programme of physical education.

04 /

Learn for living.

Make things. Express ideas. Discover interests. Practise the everyday skills that build independence and confidence.

Your questions, answered

A little more clarity.

What makes the Auremont approach distinctive?

The connection between four priorities: academic understanding, Indian roots, physical development and skills for life. The Learning Cycle provides a common teaching framework, while the Strengths Profile focuses attention on individual progress.

What is planned for the early years?

Play, stories, movement, conversation, art and hands-on exploration, with attention to language, number sense, independence and social development. Families can discuss age eligibility and the final daily routine with the admissions team.

Will activities take time away from academics?

The intention is to connect skills with academic learning, alongside dedicated opportunities for interests and practical skills. Ask for the final grade-wise timetable to see the exact balance.

What does “Indian roots” mean?

Learning through Indian and Karnataka examples, languages, stories, knowledge and culture, alongside global perspectives. The proposed approach is inclusive and welcomes families from all backgrounds.

Which classes and board details should I confirm?

The content plan covers Nursery to Grade 10 and a CBSE-oriented academic approach. Before enrolling, request the school's current affiliation details, approved classes, admission eligibility and academic-year information.

How can I arrange a campus visit?

Use the visit planner below to prepare your enquiry. Direct submission to the admissions team will be enabled when the school's official contact details are confirmed.
